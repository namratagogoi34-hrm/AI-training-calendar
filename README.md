Power BI dashboard tracking Bharat Nanochips Systems' AI training rollout (July 2026). <u><b>Training Dashboard – Bharat Nanochips Systems (July 2026)</b></u>

This Power BI dashboard tracks the AI training rollout plan for new and existing recruits at Bharat Nanochips Systems for July 2026. It consolidates training needs, costs, methods, and timelines into a single interactive view.

<u><b>Tools & process</b></u>:
Built end-to-end in Power BI Desktop. Used Power Query to clean and shape the raw training data — removing duplicates, standardizing column headers, fixing inconsistent date formats, and splitting/merging fields (e.g., combining owner names for shared responsibilities). Applied custom formatting across visuals, including conditional formatting on tables, currency formatting (₹) on cost fields, custom color themes matching brand identity, and card visuals for at-a-glance KPIs.

<u><b>Key components</b></u>:

Average Cost by Training Method (card visuals): Highlights average estimated cost across delivery formats — from Classroom lecture + discussion (₹40K) down to Case study + simulation (₹10K).
Sum of Estimated Cost by Module/Topic (bar chart): Breaks down the ₹1.55L total training budget across seven modules, led by AI Foundations (₹40K), AI Tools in Workplace (₹30K), and Basics of Digital Tools (₹30K).
Sum of Estimated Cost by Method (pie chart): Shows cost distribution across six delivery methods, with Classroom lecture (25.81%) and Hands-on workshops (19.35%) taking the largest shares.
Training Need Identified vs. First Expected Outcome (table): Maps each identified gap (e.g., AI Basics, Interdepartmental Coordination, Uneven Digital Comfort) to a measurable success metric (e.g., 90% quiz pass rate, 15% reduction in quality errors).
Master Training Plan (detail table): Full schedule listing each module, the responsible owner (Quality Head, Sales Head, IT Head, HR Head, etc.), timeline, target participant group, and training method.

<u><b>Purpose</b></u>: The dashboard gives leadership a single-page view to plan, budget, and track AI upskilling initiatives across departments — connecting training investment directly to expected business outcomes.
